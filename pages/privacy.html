Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Full translations of this legal page are in preparation. The English version currently applies.

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the GDPR is Arkem Game Studio. Contact: info@arkem-game.com.

2. What Data We Collect

When you use this website or contact us, we may collect:

  • Contact details you provide, such as name, email address, and message content submitted through the contact form or your email client.
  • Technical data automatically transmitted by your browser, such as IP address, browser type, referring URL, and time of access.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis

We process contact data to respond to your enquiries under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Technical data is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in the secure and efficient operation of the website under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

4. Data Retention

Contact enquiry data is deleted after 12 months or sooner if it is no longer needed. Technical logs are typically retained for 7 days unless a longer retention period is required for security or legal reasons.

5. Third-Party Services

This website may embed or link to third-party services including Steam, PayPal, Patreon, Ko-fi, and Discord. Each service has its own privacy policy. Please review the relevant policy before using those services.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at info@arkem-game.com to exercise any of these rights.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
